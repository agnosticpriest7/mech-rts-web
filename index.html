    Select a worker, then A / click an extractor or deposit to assign it. Select a mech, then A / click a service bay to repair. Your selection stays active. X / Alt-click inspects a building instead; CLEAR SELECTION also lets you select it with the browser cursor.

    Hold either LB or RB to open the selected unit’s actions. Keep it held, choose with the D-pad or left stick, then press A for an action or the next menu. Release without choosing to dismiss. Y toggles the same actions without holding; B backs out or clears selection. Train workers at HQ or a worker factory; build mechs at a selected foundry. Select a relay to see its 30m power radius. Click the mission heading to expand its checklist.

    Double-click a worker to select all your workers across the map, or a mech to select all your mechs of that role. Shared units count; the other pilot’s units do not. Double-tap A on the same unit does the same thing on a controller.

    1. Select workers and right click cyan alloy deposits. Workers return loads automatically. Deliver 60 alloy to complete your first objective.

    2. Select a mech and right click terrain to move, or visible hostiles to attack. Mechs spin up for 0.55 seconds, then fire both Gatling guns together while both barrel clusters keep rotating at full speed. Sound starts after DEPLOY; use SOUND ON / OFF to mute.

    3. Choose EXTRACTOR and place it on alloy. One worker assembles it, then assigned workers mine twice as quickly. Place a SENTRY on the northeast approach to defend your foothold.

    4. Press R or choose FREE REPAIR to return a mech to the service bay. Place your first SERVICE BAY for free; repairs are also free. Extra bays cost 50 alloy. The shared HQ must survive.

    5. Aliens already guard the resource seams. Escort miners to amber conductive minerals, supply power, and build a MECH FOUNDRY. Assemble a mixed reinforcement force, then destroy the hive. Expansion brings stronger retaliation. Upgrades and violet crystals are optional. Mechs fire automatically in range.

    Economy. Only delivered loads enter the shared stockpile. Cyan alloy funds basics, amber conductive minerals fund electronics, and violet crystals unlock advanced upgrades. Workers need no power to mine by hand.

    Power. Open FIELD COMMAND → POWER to view generation, demand, batteries and priorities. Keep relays linked to extend coverage. Defense, repairs and production compete for available supply; the HQ keeps emergency support. An upgrade pauses during an outage rather than spending more resources.

    Scale. M06 mechs stand 12 metres tall; worker robots are 2 metres. Zoom in for the lower inspection view. Service pads need a clear 16 × 14 metre footprint. The worker factory produces workers, not mechs.

    Upgrades. Select your own mech, open FIELD COMMAND → UPGRADES, and choose mobility, armor or weapons. The mech travels to the station and stays there while work completes. Armor and heavier weapons trade some mobility for strength.
